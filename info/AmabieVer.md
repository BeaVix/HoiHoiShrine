# Amabie Ver.

{% include image-caption.html img='amabieCover.png' alt='Cover of Amabie ver.' caption='The cover of AMABIE VER.' %}

*一撃殺虫!!ホイホイさんAMABIE VER.* (Ichigeki sacchu!! HoiHoi-San AMABIE Ver.) is a one-shot parody, taking place during an unespicified future where the COVID-19 pandemic is ongoing. It's title, AMABIE, is a reference to the yokai of the same name, which became an online trend in japanese social media, as legend says that to bring an end to a pandemic you must draw it and show it to people.

It takes place loosely somewhere during/after the events of LEGACY, with Mayu and her dad making a brief appearence, but basically every major character gets a cameo on this (except for Furuyashiki for some reason).

The basic conceit is that, because of the pandemic, the major interceptor doll brands decided to release special \'anti-COVID-19 equipped\' versions of their dolls to <s>capitalize on tragedy</s> help out society, all themed around different yokai.

{% include image-caption.html img='amabieGang.png' alt='HoiHoi-San, Pest-X-San and Combat-San anti-Covid-19 ready variants' caption='Each of them helpfully labeled with the yokai they are based-off of'%}

While this might seem simplistic enough, and the book itself is only 30-something pages, it gets divided into 3 sections, with each one telling a different story on different, unrelated, alternate universes. 

The first one is our standard HoiHoi-San chapter, showing all the drawbacks of getting a robot doll that forces you to take a covid test if it detects your temperature is a bit too high. The second one is set on a world where life-sized HoiHoi-San dolls with advanced (and opinionated) AI exists, and how they are now doing all the jobs normal people can't do. And the last one is a comic where a sentient HoiHoi-San and Pest-X-San doll are suffering the effects of quarantine on their small exterminator business.
