## What?
Ichigeki Sacchuu!! HoiHoi-San is a series that spawned from a manga serialized in 2003, created by the illustrator/mangaka Tanaka Kunihiko. Since then, it has had an anime and a videogame adaptation, a sequel, and a notable line of action figures. For more information check out: [What is ichigeki Sacchu!! HoiHoi-San?]({{site.url}}/info/hoihoisanBasics.html)

## Why?
Following the cancellation of the Tankobon release for the sequel, there has been little news of more material. Until recent years, with a sudden barrage of content: a [new edition](https://bookwalker.jp/de6712bb2b-59ea-4a92-80a8-39d20c5abb20/) of the original manga, an [one-shot spin-off]({{site.url}}/info/AmabieVer.html), re-uploads of the sequel manga, [Legacy]({{site.url}}/legacy.html), which has never been avalible outside the original serialization until now, and, of course, more action figures.

The average weeb outside Japan with a moderate interest in HoiHoi-San would be hard-pressed in becoming aware of these developments. That's why I hope for this site to bring attention to the fact that <b>HoiHoi-San is still alive and kicking</b> (for the most part).

And, given the series state of semi-obscurity, and being more than 20 years old at this point, a lot of content has been lost to time or is outside the reach of those not living in Japan. That's why this site also serves as a collection of most things related to HoiHoi-San.
