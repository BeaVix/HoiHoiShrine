Legacy was(/is?) the sequel manga to the original HoiHoi-San. For more information, I highly reccomend you check out the [LEGACY]({{site.url}}/info/legacy.html) page on the info section.

Since its hard to get by on the internet and in real life. I have uploaded what I could find of it. This is not a complete list of all the chapters, but someday maybe it will be.

It's separated on 'Original' and 'Revised' versions. On Original are just the chapters as they where published. Revised are re-uploads by the author with updated art and slight story corrections.

Since the revised chapters weren't published in any particular order, and the lack of completion of the original run makes it hard to order them chronologically, they have been grouped loosely by shared themes.

The chinese scanlation was made and published by the scanlator group SOSG. Originally found on [ACGN](https://comic.acgn.cc/view-58719.htm).
